Speak an answer with the mic, or upload a recording. Shaded areas are pauses of 250 ms or longer.

Acoustic markers are computed locally from the audio signal (energy-based speech/pause detection and autocorrelation pitch tracking). Detected pauses also count towards the pause marker. Voice transcription uses your browser’s speech-recognition service, which may send audio to its provider (e.g. Google in Chrome). In this preview the microphone may be blocked: download the HTML file and open it in Chrome to use voice, or upload an audio file.
